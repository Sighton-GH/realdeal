## Goal
Display pieces: the progress bar, price typography, and loading skeletons.

## Contract
- `ProgressBar(props: { value: number /*0..1*/; tone?: "brand" | VerdictTier; height?: 12 | 16; label?; className? })`
- `PriceText(props: { amount: number; unit?: Unit; size?: "sm" | "md" | "lg" | "xl"; strike?; className? })`
- `Skeleton(props: { className? })`

## Requirements
**ProgressBar**: `rounded-full bg-sunken` track; filled part in the tone face colour with a lighter highlight band along its top third (a flat `bg-white/35` rounded strip inset 4px from the ends, not a gradient). Width animates with `spring.pop` (motion). `role="progressbar"` with aria values and `aria-label`.

**PriceText**: `font-display tabular` bold. Sizes: sm 16px, md 22px, lg 32px, xl 56px (64px at md+). The `$` renders at 60% size and is raised (Kahoot score style); cents keep full size. `unit` appends `/kg` etc. in Nunito 700 small ink-soft. `strike`: ink-soft text with a 2px diagonal line drawn over it (an absolutely positioned rotated bar), not `text-decoration`. Use `formatMoney` for the number and split the `$` off.

**Skeleton**: `bg-sunken rounded-sm`, opacity pulse 0.6↔1 over 1.2s; static under reduced motion. `aria-hidden`.

## Acceptance
- [ ] PriceText looks like a game-show score at xl and is still clean at sm.
- [ ] ProgressBar animates smoothly; respects reduced motion.
