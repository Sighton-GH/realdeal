## Goal
The app shell: phone column on a grape stage, top bar, bottom nav with the raised Scan button, and the background pattern.

## Contract
- `AppShell()` (layout route: renders `<Outlet/>`), `TopBar(props: { title?; back?; right? })`, `BottomNav()`, `AppColumn({ children; className? })`
- Keep `TopBarContext` / `useTopBar` exactly as they are (you don't return that file). AppShell must keep: providing `TopBarContext`, mounting `ToastProvider`, hiding the bottom nav on `/check/:itemId` and `/scan`, hiding the top bar when `hidden` is set.
- AppColumn must keep `id="app-column"` and `relative overflow-hidden` on the column (Sheet portals into it).

## Requirements
**AppColumn**: mobile = full screen `h-dvh`. ≥768px: page `bg-grape-900` with `/pattern.svg` repeating, column `max-w-[460px] rounded-xl`, height `calc(100dvh - 48px)`, 24px vertical margin, 4px grape-700 bottom lip (solid `box-shadow: 0 4px 0`). Column is a flex column; the main area scrolls inside it so the nav stays pinned.

**TopBar**: 56px, canvas, 2px bottom `line`. Left: back IconButton (`ArrowLeft`, `navigate(-1)`, or `/check` if there's no history) or `<Logo size="sm"/>` linking to `/check`. Centre: optional title, Fredoka 600 18px, truncates. Right: custom node, or the sound toggle (`SpeakerHigh` / `SpeakerSlash`, wired to `soundOn`/`toggleSound` in the store, label "Turn sound off"/"Turn sound on"). Safe-area top padding on mobile.

**BottomNav**: 72px + safe-area, canvas, 2px top `line`. Check (`Tag`) and Tricks (`Detective`) as icon + label NavLinks; centre Scan = 64px `bg-grape-500` circle with grape-700 `.press` lip, raised 16px above the bar, white `Scan` icon 30px, label "Scan" below in grape-500. Active: grape-500, `fill` icon, Nunito 800; inactive: ink-soft, bold icon, Nunito 700. Check is active on `/check` and `/check/*` and `/item/*`.

**pattern.svg**: 160x160 tile, about 6 small glyphs (coins: circle with inner ring; price tags: rounded pentagon with a hole), white strokes at about 5% opacity, scattered and slightly rotated. Subtle: it should read as texture from a distance.

- [ ] Bottom nav stays pinned while content scrolls; hidden on price entry and scan.
